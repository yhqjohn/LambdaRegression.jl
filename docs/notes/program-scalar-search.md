# Program-guided search

The ordinary lambda and function-definition AST is authoritative for semantics,
lexical sharing, inspection, and complexity. Scalar regions and frozen typed
contexts are derived views whose validity depends on the complete program and
its task world.

Fixed-context scalar sessions retain their populations and archives across
bounded local actions. A changed enclosing context requires fresh state;
structural similarity alone does not authorize reuse.

Joint search instead retains complete programs in fixed-capacity islands.
Local scalar variation and atomic region-group exchange can change several
parts of one candidate; global typed variation can change the enclosing
structure. Shared constants remain shared during transport and can be refined
against the complete objective. Every changed candidate is checked and scored
as a whole before admission.

Region transport requires compatible enclosing syntax, dependencies, and
materials. Observational structure buckets do not establish substitutability.
Structural edits rebuild derived views, and all live-member capacity belongs
to the task rather than to individual regions or templates.

The workflow alternates bounded joint development with outer program search.
Transfers between these engines are validated and re-scored by the receiver;
unchanged same-task island migrants can retain their scores. Structural,
numerical, and transfer costs have explicit limits and accounting. Published
programs are read-only, and the task world remains stable during a session.
